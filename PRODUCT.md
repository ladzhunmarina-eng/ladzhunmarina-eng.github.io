# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Product design leads, hiring managers and recruiters who judge craft and taste in a couple of minutes, mostly on a desktop browser. They skim first (hero, featured work, one case study) and read closely only if something earns it.

## Product Purpose

A static product-design portfolio for Marina, a product designer since 2015. It shows her best work as cases, her experience, what colleagues say, and how she works, and it has to prove the craft by being well made itself. Success is a visitor who opens a case study and sends a message.

## Brand Personality

Confident and understated. Warm cream and one soft yellow, big plain type, generous space, and a few small human touches (a waving hand, letters that drop in) rather than decoration. The work and the writing carry the page; the interface stays out of their way.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / template pages: identical card grids, big-number hero metrics, gradient accents.
- Dark neon / techy: black backgrounds with glowing accents and monospace as a costume.

## Design Principles

1. Craft is the proof: details are exact (alignment, spacing, states), because the site is itself a design sample.
2. Understated over loud: one idea per fold, a restrained palette, motion only where it adds meaning.
3. Typography is the voice: one family, committed weight and size contrast, no decorative extras.
4. Warm and human: small, specific touches of personality beat generic polish.
5. Static, fast and honest: real content and real screenshots, no tricks that cost speed or clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA: visible focus, enough contrast, semantic HTML, alt text, keyboard-reachable everything. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for every animation. Content stays readable and unclipped from 320px to wide desktops.
